Before ANY spend on paid APIs (image generation on OpenAI or Gemini/Nano Banana, Higgsfield credits, judge/vision calls, re-dos and fixes), ask Shoaib first, every time. Say:
- exactly what will be made (which posts, how many images);
- which model;
- the estimated cost in PKR, at a stated rate, with USD in brackets.

Then wait for his yes. A re-do or fix after review is a NEW spend and needs a new yes. Free calls (key checks, listing models, reading KB) don't need asking.

**Why:** Shoaib, 2026-10-06, while adding Gemini credits: "har bar mujse pochy baghair spend nai kerna Pehly pochna hai mujse". On 2026-10-05 the OpenAI $5.98 ran out after many runs, fixes and judge calls that each cost money. My estimates were also too low (judge calls and retries were left out).

**How to apply:**
- Ask with a precise plan and a realistic estimate that includes the judge and the likely retries.
- After his yes, spend only what was approved.
- If something needs fixing, report it and ask again.
- This builds on [[feedback-no-batch-without-ask]] and [[feedback-prices-in-pkr]].
