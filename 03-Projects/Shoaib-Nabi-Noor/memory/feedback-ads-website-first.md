Shoaib's rule (2026-10-06): for the hotel clients (Silver Sand, Elegant), ads must not open WhatsApp or a phone call directly. Every ad goes to the website or landing page first. People WhatsApp or call from the site after seeing the rooms and prices.

**Why:** without the website, people message or call with no idea of price or location, and the hotel gets junk leads.

**How to apply:**
- Meta: optimise website campaigns on the pixel CONTACT event, never use click-to-WhatsApp.
- Google: no CALL or BUSINESS_MESSAGE assets, at campaign or account level.
- The site's WhatsApp links still carry the Ref code (utm_content), so the source stays visible.
