Stage the product differently on every post, not only the background. The same round podium or stone under the bottle every time is a failure. A podium is allowed at most once in 6 posts.

Staging library, from KB global rule `product-presentation-variety` (2026-10-06):
- in the scene (on the litter, at the drinker line);
- on a work surface with one honest prop;
- with its use (water tank, feeder, egg tray);
- natural still-life (herbs, eggs, grains);
- floating, upright;
- window or frame break;
- pack family (only when real photos of both sizes exist);
- podium (rare).

**Why:** Shoaib, 2026-10-06, on the Meezab Aminoreef Sol brand sample: "har product ka 1 hi style ata hai K bottle 1 Round Stone ya jo bhi shape hai us per rakhi hui har bar product ka bhi kuch naya style hona chaiye". He liked the post otherwise.

**How to apply:**
- Name the staging per post in the plan, matched to the topic. Never repeat the previous post's staging.
- The pack stays the real photo, identical: no tilt, no rotation, nothing covering it.
- Show the plan before any spend ([[feedback-ask-before-spend]]).
