**Rule:** never put emojis in captions. This covers every project and every part of a caption: the hook, the body, contact lines (write "Call:", "Location:", not 📞 📍) and Jummah or occasion posts. Hashtags stay.

**Why:** Shoaib, 2026-11-02, right after the Danish 2–10 Nov captions went out with an emoji on each hook: "captions mai imojis waghaira na unse kiya kero professional bas". He wants client pages to read as professional.

**How to apply:**
- End the hook line with a full stop instead of an emoji.
- It is saved as KB global rule `captions-no-emojis`, which wins over any project style guide that asks for emojis. Ask him before making an exception.
- On 2026-11-02 the 8 Danish captions were rewritten without emojis. 58 other upcoming scheduled feed captions still had emojis (Elegant 27, Silver Sand 11, Avenza Land 9, Avalon 5, Choppers 3, Eventia 2, Meezab 1). Shoaib said to leave them as they are ("Rehny do … next se"), so don't touch already-scheduled captions. The rule applies to new captions only.
- Older caption-style notes that list emoji lines are outdated on this point, e.g. the contact block in [[aijaz-danish-brands]].
