Shoaib Nabi Noor is a performance marketing specialist in Multan who runs Ads by Shoaib (adsbyshoaib.com). The site's dashboard also runs his clients' social posting, GBP replies, invoices and retainers.

**Language:**
- Reply in **Roman Urdu** (an Urdu/English mix), short and plain.
- Anything client-facing (posts, captions, invoices, emails) is in plain English unless he says otherwise. See [[feedback-plain-english]].

**How he works:**
- Short messages, often a screenshot with a red circle and one line ("ye pdf print mai araha hai"). Read the screenshot closely; the circle is the ask.
- He often asks for status ("Posts ka status batao") and wants a straight answer with numbers.
- When he gives a direction in one line, do that and show the result. For layout/UX restructuring, describe the design first ([[shoaib-confirm-ux-before-building]]). When the ask is ambiguous after one wrong guess, offer two previews with AskUserQuestion; that worked well on the invoice totals (2026-10-26).
- "ruk jao" means stop at once, start nothing new, and keep the work ready.
- He wants changes committed, pushed and live quickly ("Commit Push live kero jaldi"). Never force-push; he works from two PCs ([[shoaib-two-pc-workflow]]).

**Skills:** for website design, copy and SEO work he wants his own "-by shoaib" skills: premium-web-design-by-shoaib, premium-website-builder-by-shoaib, copywriting-mastery-by-shoaib, website-seo-by-shoaib. Don't use portfolio-website-builder-by-shoaib here: its locked dark design and HTML-zip output don't fit this Next.js light site.

**Model:** in August 2026 he switched models for the site build himself. The model is his choice; don't assume one.
