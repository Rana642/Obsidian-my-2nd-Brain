Shoaib writes in Roman Urdu ("kero jaisy behter lagy", "Aisa banao") and
delegates the approach rather than choosing between options. When offered a
numbered menu of next steps, the reply was "do whichever you think is better."

**Why:** Presenting options and waiting costs a round trip they did not want.
They are directing outcomes, not implementation choices.

**How to apply:** Make the routine call, state the assumption in one line, and
build. Reserve questions for things that would waste real work if guessed
wrong. Reference material often arrives as screenshots mid-turn — treat those
as the spec for structure and mechanics, and reconcile them against
[[anisa-herbal-claims-discipline]] rather than copying wholesale.
